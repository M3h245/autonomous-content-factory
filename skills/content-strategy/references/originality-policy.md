# Originality and anti-template policy
Why: YouTube treats mass-produced, templated, low-variation content as "inauthentic" and reviews whole channels.
Every idea needs a value-add a viewer would notice, for example:
- answers the follow-up question others skip; corrects a common misconception with evidence;
- a demonstration or visual model that makes the mechanism obvious; original synthesis of 2+ sources;
- a local or audience-specific angle; a comparison with numbers; a story with a real stake.
Batch rules: ≥2 formats per batch of 3+, distinct angles (MMR does this), novelty ≥0.35 vs history.
Across batches rotate: palette/visual style, caption style, voice, music bed, structure template, title pattern.
Never: reupload near-duplicates, swap only the topic noun in the same script, or pad with filler to reach length.
