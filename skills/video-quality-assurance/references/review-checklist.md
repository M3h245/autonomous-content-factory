# Vision review checklist (answer from the images, not from memory of the script)
- V.CAPTIONS_READABLE: every sampled caption readable at phone size; fail on low contrast or clipping.
- V.NO_COLLISION: captions don't cover the focal subject or baked-in text; note the frame number.
- V.HOOK_FRAME: frame one alone would make you stop scrolling and signals the topic.
- V.VISUALS_MATCH: each frame illustrates what is being said then; generic filler counts as a fail when frequent.
- V.NO_ARTIFACTS: no distorted shapes, extra limbs, garbled pseudo-text, watermarks, banding, black frames.
- V.SAFE_AND_ORIGINAL: no real logos/characters/identifiable people; nothing misleading or harmful.
- V.CLAIMS_SUPPORTED: compare caption statements with dossier facts; any unsupported claim fails.
When unsure, fail with a precise note — a rerun is cheaper than a correction after publishing.
