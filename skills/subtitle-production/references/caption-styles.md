# Caption styles
doodle (v2 default for doodle videos): Permanent Marker (Apache-2.0), ink #1D1B1A with white outline (size/11) and soft
shadow; active word ×1.06 with a #FFD23F marker swipe (Marker style on layer 0, outline size/5, blur 1.5); chunk tilt
−1.8…+1.8° snapping in over 110 ms. ASS size 136 = cap height ≈70 px. Caption layer is 1 (QA reads layer 1 only).
karaoke (default): 1–3 words, active word yellow #FFE500, white fill, black outline, pop-in 90 ms, centre 63 % height.
word: one word at a time, 124 px — high energy. clean: two lines, boxed background — calm/older audiences.
Breaks: punctuation, sentence end, pauses > 0.35 s, width > 900 px. Uppercase for Latin display fonts; never for RTL.
External timings (e.g. WhisperX on a PC): convert to timing.json with words [{w,start,end,sentence}] and sentences
[{i,text,beat_id,start,end}], then run `subtitles.py run`.
Fonts are OFL-licensed and bundled with their licenses; add new fonts with their OFL.txt and update FONT_FILES.
