# Hook archetypes (spoken ≤ ~3 s; on-screen ≤ 6 words and adds information)
| Archetype | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| contradiction | "X should be Y. It isn't." | The sky should actually look violet. |
| curiosity_gap | withhold the key fact | Your eyes are hiding the sky's real color. |
| myth_bust | name the belief, deny it | Blue light isn't scattered the most. |
| specific_number | concrete figure up front | Violet scatters nine times more than red. |
| stakes | consequence for the viewer | This mistake ruins every sunset photo. |
| question | a question the viewer can't answer yet | Why isn't the sky purple? |
| comparison | two things in tension | Physics says violet. Your eyes say blue. |
| pov | "POV: …" situational | POV: you finally see the sky's real color. |
| before_after | change over time | Same sky, same sun, completely different color. |
| story_open | in medias res | In 1871, a physicist explained the sky. |
| warning | caution with a reason | Don't trust your eyes on this one. |
| challenge | invite a test | Look up and count the colors. You'll miss one. |
Avoid: greetings, "in this video", "did you know", unpayable promises, rage bait, fake urgency.
